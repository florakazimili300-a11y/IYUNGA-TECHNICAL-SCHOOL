OUR BACKGROUND

A School Focused on Academic and Technical Excellence

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a government secondary school located in Mbeya City Council, Mbeya. The institution provides secondary education together with technical and vocational skills.

Our approach is designed to help students develop theoretical knowledge, practical abilities, creativity, discipline and problem-solving skills.
